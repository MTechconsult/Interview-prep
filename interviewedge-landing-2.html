How it works

The STAR method, broken into a simple four-part structure

Every strong interview answer follows the same shape. InterviewEdge walks you through building it, one question at a time.

S

Situation

Set the scene — where you were and what was going on.

T

Task

What was your specific responsibility or goal.

A

Action

What you did, step by step — the heart of your answer.

R

Result

What happened, ideally with a clear outcome or number.

See it in action

Here's what a completed answer looks like

Question: "Describe a time you solved a difficult problem with limited resources."

Situation: Our regional office needed to run a customer outreach campaign, but the quarter's budget had already been committed to a larger initiative.

Task: I needed to still reach a meaningful number of customers without any new spend approved.

Action: I pulled our existing customer data to build a smaller, more targeted list, and used automation features already built into our current tools instead of requesting anything new.

Result: We reached most of the original target audience at zero additional cost, and it became our default approach for tight-budget campaigns going forward.
